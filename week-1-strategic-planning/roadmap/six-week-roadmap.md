# Six-Week Roadmap

| Week | Focus | Main Output | Dependency |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Strategic planning | Strategy, use cases, KPI framework | None |
| 2 | Data sourcing and preparation | Source register, cleaned dataset | Week 1 requirements |
| 3 | Data modelling and DAX | Model, relationships, measures | Week 2 processed data |
| 4 | Dashboard design | Interactive Power BI dashboard | Week 3 model |
| 5 | Predictive analytics | Evaluated model and output, if feasible | Week 2 data and Week 3 model |
| 6 | Final evaluation | Final report, consolidated dashboard and website | Weeks 1–5 |

The roadmap is subject to data availability and the feasibility of
the proposed analysis.
