# Proposed Use Cases

## 1. Project Performance Monitoring
Purpose: Understand the distribution of projects by status and
available progress indicators.

Questions:
- How many projects are recorded?
- What statuses are represented?
- How does project status vary by sector or state?

## 2. Cost and Expenditure Analysis
Purpose: Examine approved project cost and reported expenditure.

Questions:
- What is the total approved cost?
- What expenditure is reported?
- How does expenditure compare across sectors?

## 3. Schedule Analysis
Purpose: Explore project dates and scheduled completion information.

Questions:
- Which projects have scheduled completion dates?
- How are completion dates distributed?
- Are revised dates available for comparison?

## 4. Sector and Regional Analysis
Purpose: Compare available project indicators across sectors and states.

Questions:
- Which sectors have the largest number of projects?
- How are approved costs distributed?
- How does the dataset vary geographically?

## 5. Risk Exploration
Purpose: Explore potential indicators for further review.

Possible indicators:
- Missing or inconsistent dates
- Large differences between approved cost and expenditure
- Reported project status
- Available schedule changes

These indicators are not validated risk scores. Any risk classification
would require a documented methodology and suitable evidence.
