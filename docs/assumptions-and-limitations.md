# Assumptions and Limitations

## Data Assumptions
- Publicly available records are used as supplied by their publishers.
- Source-reported dates and costs are not independently audited.
- A blank value is treated as missing unless the source defines it
  otherwise.
- Different projects may have different reporting frequencies.

## Analytical Assumptions
- KPI calculations will be based only on available and documented fields.
- Comparisons will use consistent units and definitions.
- A project's reported status will not automatically be interpreted
  as its actual on-site condition.

## Limitations
- Public data may not include daily construction progress.
- Labour hours and workforce productivity may not be available.
- Material usage and contractor-level costs may not be available.
- A cross-sectional dataset may not support time-series forecasting.
- Predictive performance may be limited by sample size, missing values
  and the availability of historical outcomes.

## Responsible Interpretation
BuildSight BI outputs are educational analytical results. They should
not be interpreted as official project ratings or definitive forecasts.
